The nine organization names and their order follow the supplied `Trusted Partners & Clients .pdf`. Names use a darker green and a larger size, alongside full-color logos where an identity could be confirmed.

The strip pauses on hover. Keyboard focus stops the animation and allows horizontal scrolling. Reduced-motion preferences disable the animation. A duplicate list makes the scrolling loop seamless; it is hidden from assistive technology and its links are excluded from the tab order.
